Does ₹2,999/month cover the ad budget?
No — ₹2,999/month is our management fee only. It covers campaign setup, targeting, Pixel/CAPI configuration, bid optimization, and weekly reports. Your ad budget is paid separately and directly to Meta via your own Business Manager account.
How much experience do you have with Meta Ads?
Our team has 2+ years of hands-on Meta performance marketing experience with over ₹50 Lakhs+ in total ad spend managed across e-commerce, local service businesses, real estate, and D2C brands.
How fast will my campaigns go live?
Once you subscribe, we begin the onboarding audit immediately. Pixel/CAPI configuration and campaign structure are completed within 24 business hours.

You (the client) provide the ad images, videos, and copy. We advise on the exact formats, dimensions, and messaging angles that perform best based on our data and your audience.
